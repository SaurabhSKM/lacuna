Aria Cloud Storage - Billing and Refund Policy

All Aria plans are billed monthly or annually. Annual billing gives a 15%
discount compared to the monthly rate.

Refunds: Customers on monthly plans may request a full refund within 14
days of their initial charge if they have used less than 5 GB of storage.
After 14 days, no refunds are issued for monthly plans, but customers may
cancel at any time to stop future charges.

Customers on annual plans may request a pro-rated refund for unused months
within the first 60 days of the billing cycle, provided the account has not
exceeded its Starter-tier storage limit (100 GB) during that period.

Enterprise plan refunds are handled case-by-case under the terms of the
signed Enterprise agreement and are not covered by this policy.

Failed payments result in a 7-day grace period before the account is
downgraded to a read-only state. Accounts remain read-only for 30 days
before data deletion begins.
