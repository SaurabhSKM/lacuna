Aria Cloud Storage - Support and Service Level Agreement

Support channels

All plans include email support at support@aria.example. Starter customers
receive a first response within two business days. Team customers receive a
first response within eight business hours and gain access to live chat
during the hours of 09:00 to 18:00 UTC on weekdays. Enterprise customers
receive a first response within one hour for incidents classified as
Severity 1, and are assigned a named technical account manager.

Severity definitions

Severity 1 means the service is completely unavailable, or customer data is
inaccessible, for all users in an organisation. Severity 2 means a core
feature such as file synchronisation or sharing is unavailable while the
rest of the service functions. Severity 3 covers degraded performance and
partial feature failures. Severity 4 covers questions, documentation
requests, and cosmetic defects.

Uptime commitment

Aria commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Team plans and 99.95% monthly
uptime for Enterprise plans. The Starter plan carries no contractual uptime
commitment. Uptime is measured as the percentage of one-minute intervals in
a calendar month during which the synchronisation API responded
successfully to Aria's own monitoring probes. Scheduled maintenance
announced at least 72 hours in advance is excluded from the calculation.

Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the committed figure, affected customers may
claim a service credit against the following month's invoice: 10% for
uptime between 99.0% and the commitment, 25% for uptime between 95.0% and
99.0%, and 50% for uptime below 95.0%. Credits must be requested in writing
within 30 days of the end of the affected month. Credits are the sole
remedy for missed uptime and are not paid out in cash.

Escalation

A Severity 1 incident that remains unresolved after four hours is escalated
to the on-call engineering manager automatically. Enterprise customers may
additionally request escalation through their technical account manager at
any severity level.
