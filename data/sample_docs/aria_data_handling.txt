Aria Cloud Storage - Data Handling and Retention

Storage regions

Customer file data is stored in one of two regions, selected when the
organisation is created: eu-west (Dublin) or us-east (Virginia). The region
cannot be changed after creation; moving an organisation between regions
requires a migration performed by Aria support and is available only to
Enterprise customers. Account metadata such as user names and email
addresses is replicated to both regions for authentication purposes.

Retention and deletion

Deleted files are moved to a recoverable trash area for 30 days on Starter
and Team plans, and for 90 days on the Enterprise plan. After that window
the file is purged from primary storage within 24 hours and from backups
within 35 days. A deletion request submitted through the admin console
covers both primary storage and backups, and Aria issues a written
confirmation once the backup purge completes.

Account closure

When an organisation closes its account, data remains available in a
read-only state for 30 days, after which deletion begins automatically.
Customers may request immediate deletion instead, which skips the read-only
period.

Backups

Primary storage is replicated synchronously within a region across three
availability zones. A separate daily backup is retained for 35 days.
Point-in-time restore to any hour within the last seven days is available
on the Enterprise plan. Starter and Team customers may request a restore
from the most recent daily backup through support.

Regulatory posture

Aria processes personal data as a processor under the EU General Data
Protection Regulation and offers a data processing addendum to all
customers on request. Aria also honours consumer rights requests under the
California Consumer Privacy Act. Aria publishes a list of subprocessors and
notifies customers 30 days before adding a new one.

Law enforcement requests

Aria requires valid legal process for any disclosure of customer content,
notifies the affected customer unless legally prohibited, and publishes
aggregate request counts twice a year in a transparency report.
